Public Functions

variant()

Default constructor: zero normal, direction, Schmid tensor and transformation strain.

variant(arma::vec n, arma::vec m, double g)

Constructor with parameters; builds the Schmid tensor and the transformation strain.

Parameters:

  • n – habit plane normal (size 3)

  • m – transformation direction (size 3)

  • g – transformation magnitude, \( \varepsilon^{T}_n = g\,\mathrm{Schmid\_v}(n, m) \)

Copy constructor.

Destructor.
